The page DOM exposes individual official portrait URLs for the Patron, Anupam Trivedi, Aparna Mishra, all fourteen Central Advisory members, and all three Digital Trainers. All 20 original files were downloaded directly from the official source and uploaded unchanged as managed project assets.

The original assets vary substantially in resolution. The largest leadership originals are Anupam Trivedi at 1016×1236 and Aparna Mishra at 960×1280. The updated card layout therefore avoids the former visual softening filter and keeps source images inside a bounded, image-native presentation rather than enlarging low-resolution originals unnecessarily.
