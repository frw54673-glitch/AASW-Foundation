# Smile Foundation Leadership Reference

Reference reviewed on 18 August 2026: https://www.smilefoundationindia.org/leadership/

## Transferable visual principles

The reference presents people as the primary content. It uses a clear leadership heading, generous white space, one prominent portrait-and-biography unit at a time, and an ordered progression from trustees to mentor and management team. Portraits are treated as recognisable headshots rather than decorative crops.

## AASW adaptation boundary

The AASW redesign will adopt the high-level pattern only: a larger face-forward Leadership presentation, readable identity hierarchy and deliberate spacing. It will retain AASW's existing warm civic-editorial palette, typography, official source portraits and verified team data; no Smile Foundation copy, logo, assets or team information will be reused.

## AASW adaptation QA

The updated AASW Team page was reviewed at 1280×900 and 375×812. Leadership now presents as a dedicated three-card, large portrait row; Central Advisory and Digital Trainers use a readable three-column people grid at desktop and collapse to a single face-forward stream on mobile. Official portrait sources, the existing filter, keyboard focus treatment and reduced-motion behavior remain intact.

## Central Advisory enhancement QA

Central Advisory now follows the same large, face-forward card scale as Leadership. At desktop it uses a spacious three-column grid; each official portrait is rendered in the face-safe frame so no face is cropped. At mobile it becomes a one-card-per-row presentation with the same clear image scale and text hierarchy.

## Digital Trainer enhancement QA

Digital Trainers now use the same large face-forward presentation as Leadership and Central Advisory. Desktop maintains a three-card image hierarchy; mobile stacks one trainer per card with a clear, uncropped face-safe frame. The official Trainer portraits and their verified names remain unchanged.
