# AASW Foundation — About Page Content Map

This implementation reference is derived from the user-supplied AASW website source archive. The public About page should preserve the source meaning and institutional claims while presenting them in the approved editorial design system.

## About Us

> “The chance for growth, leadership, and groundbreaking ideas belongs to everyone in today's fast-moving world—especially women from disadvantaged communities. Yet, deep-seated barriers still stop countless Indian women from finding their footing in technology, business, and sustainable careers.”

> “AASW Foundation was founded to change this reality. We're a human-centered organization in the heart of Uttar Pradesh, dedicated to bridging the divide in digital business ownership.”

## Objective and Aim

## How We Work

> “We use a sustainable and scalable model, combining local knowledge with global standards.”

The five source steps are market understanding, identifying local challenges and changemakers, tailored digital training and mentorship for women entrepreneurs, environmental responsibility in every project, community and advisory collaboration, and scaling successful initiatives through data, feedback and state-level promotion.

## Team and Governance

The source team page identifies the patron as **Pujya Mahant Shri Shiv Chetnanand Saraswati Ji Maharaj (Khadeshwari Maharaj)**. It names **Anupam Trivedi** as a dedicated social reformer using digital technology to enable financial independence for rural women in Uttar Pradesh. It names **Aparna Mishra** as bringing community-building, leadership-development and strategic-partnership experience with a focus on gender equality and green business practices.

The source also lists the following AASW people: Prof. Meera K. Desai, Anand Vardhan Shukla, Dr. Archana Raj Singh, Dr. Priya Vasist, Dr. Mamta Tiwari, Dr. Navneesh Tyagi, Dr. Seema Dwivedi, Kushal Pal Singh, Madhuri Joshi, Mamta Jain, Manisha Tripathi, Pro. M. Moni, Anuj Kanawat and Anupam Shahi.

The source governance structure includes the Central Advisory Council, General Body, Board of Directors, Executive Committee and Programs Division. Its listed policy themes are transparency, financial auditing, anti-corruption, conflict of interest, whistleblower protection and data privacy.
